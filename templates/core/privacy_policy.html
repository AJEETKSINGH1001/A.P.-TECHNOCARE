Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how A.P. TECHNOCARE handles information when you visit our website or contact our business.

Last updated: 3 October 2026

1. Introduction

A.P. TECHNOCARE ("we", "our" or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how information may be collected, used and handled when you access our website, submit an enquiry or communicate with us.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with our website, we may collect the following information:

  • Name and contact details.
  • Company or organisation name.
  • Email address and telephone number.
  • Product requirements and enquiry details.
  • Information submitted through contact or quotation forms.
  • Basic technical information, such as browser type and website usage data, where collected by our website or tools.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information provided to us may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries and quotation requests.
  • Provide product and service information.
  • Communicate about orders and business requirements.
  • Improve our website and customer experience.
  • Maintain business records.
  • Meet applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not publish your personal information as part of our normal business operations. Information may be shared with relevant service providers, business partners or authorities when necessary to respond to your request, operate our services or comply with applicable legal requirements.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures intended to protect information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or loss. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website or its supporting technologies may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, remember preferences or understand website usage. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including business operations, record-keeping and applicable legal requirements.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information held by us. You may also raise questions or concerns about how your information is handled by contacting us using the details below.

9. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites. Please review their privacy policies separately.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any revised version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the handling of your information, please contact A.P. TECHNOCARE.
